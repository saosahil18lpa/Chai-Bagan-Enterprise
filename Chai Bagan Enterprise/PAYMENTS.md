# Payment provider planning

There is no production payment connection in this static site. A merchant account, verified business onboarding, a server endpoint, server-side product prices, and private API credentials are required before checkout can securely take money.

Cashfree currently advertises a promotion for eligible new merchants that offers zero payment gateway fees on the first ₹20 lakh of GMV, with an offer end date of 31 March 2027; eligibility and terms apply. After any promotion, transaction fees apply. Razorpay advertises no setup or annual maintenance charges on its standard plan, while successful transactions carry payment fees. Neither should be described as unconditionally free. Check provider terms before choosing.

References: [Cashfree pricing](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-gateway-charges/) · [Razorpay pricing](https://razorpay.com/pricing/)
