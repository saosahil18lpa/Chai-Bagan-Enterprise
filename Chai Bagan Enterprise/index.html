What kinds of tea do you offer?+

Our sample collection features Assam CTC, orthodox leaf, tea dust, premium leaf, spiced blends, and green tea. Ask us what’s currently available.

Which tea would you recommend for everyday chai?+

Assam CTC is our suggested starting point for a strong, familiar cup with milk. Tell us how you brew and we can help you choose.

Can I order wholesale for my business?+

Yes, we welcome wholesale and retail enquiries. Get in touch with what you need and our team will confirm suitable options.

What pack sizes and prices are available?+

Pack sizes, stock, and pricing can vary. We confirm current details directly so you receive an accurate quote before ordering.

Do you deliver outside Vadodara?+

We’re based in Undera, Vadodara. Contact us with your city and quantity and we’ll confirm delivery availability.
